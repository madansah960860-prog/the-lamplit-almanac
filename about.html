About The Lamplit Almanac

By the The Lamplit Almanac Editorial Team · Last reviewed: October 2026

The Lamplit Almanac is an independent journal of history and general knowledge, made for the simple, lasting pleasure of learning. It takes its name and its spirit from the old almanacs and the long winter evenings when, by lamplight, a good book was the best of company. Our aim is to be exactly that kind of company — unhurried, warm, and endlessly curious.

What we do

We write long, plain-spoken articles on the history of America and the world, portraits of historical figures worth remembering, and clear explanations of how the everyday world came to work as it does. And we keep an "on this day" collection, a note for the days of the year in the old almanac tradition. Everything is meant to be read for pleasure, at whatever pace suits you.

How we write our history

Accuracy and fairness are our watchwords. Every article is written in our own words — we reproduce no copyrighted text, and we quote only from works long in the public domain. We check our facts against reliable references, we present events evenhandedly, and we are frank about the complexities and the darker chapters of the past, for honest history includes them. Drafts may be prepared with AI assistance and are reviewed and edited by our editorial team before publishing, and every page carries a "Last reviewed" date.

What we do not do

The Lamplit Almanac is an independent journal of history and general knowledge. It is free from top to bottom, sells nothing, and carries no advertising. We take no partisan side and push no agenda; our only loyalty is to the facts, honestly told. The Lamplit Almanac is an independent educational website and is not affiliated with any institution, publisher, or society mentioned.

Our standing promises

We keep a few plain promises. We will write in our own words and check our facts with care. We will tell history evenhandedly, including its difficult chapters, and take no partisan side. The pages will stay free, uncluttered, and large-typed, with nothing to buy and no advertising to distract from the reading. And your visit will be yours alone — we gather nothing about you beyond the text size you choose, which never leaves your own device. These are the promises of an honest almanac, and we mean to keep them.

About the pictures

The images here are openly licensed pictures from public collections, downloaded and kept on our own pages rather than borrowed from across the web. Where a license asks for credit, we name the source beneath the picture, and a full list lives in the site's image credits file.

Write to the keeper

We love letters from fellow readers — a subject you would like us to cover, a fact you can add, a memory a story stirred, or a correction we ought to make. Reader letters genuinely shape what we write next, and they are among the great pleasures of keeping this almanac. Write any time to letters@lamplitalmanac.com, and see our Contact page for what to expect.
