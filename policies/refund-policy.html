Refund Policy

By the The Lamplit Almanac Editorial Team · Last reviewed: October 2026

Effective date: October 20, 2026. The following refund policy governs your use of The Lamplit Almanac.

The short version: The Lamplit Almanac is free, nothing is for sale, and so there is nothing to refund. Here is the longer version, and an important safety note.

Everything here is free

The Lamplit Almanac does not sell products or services, does not take payments, and does not ask for your card details. Since we never charge you, there is nothing for us to refund.

We mention this plainly because clarity protects you. When a website takes no payments at all, you never have to wonder whether a bill, a renewal, or a “membership fee” from it could be real. With The Lamplit Almanac, the answer is always no, and knowing that with certainty is itself a small form of protection against the scams that prey on confusion about who charged what.

If that ever changes — if The Lamplit Almanac were one day to offer something you could buy — we would spell out the price, the terms, and the refund window on this page before a single payment was ever possible. Nothing would be hidden, and nothing would be charged without your clear, deliberate choice.

If we ever offer something paid

If The Lamplit Almanac ever offers a paid item in the future — for instance, a printed workbook — a 30-day refund window would apply, and we would update this page with the full details before any sale took place. Until you see that change here, assume everything is free.

Why we keep it free

People sometimes ask how The Lamplit Almanac can give everything away. The honest answer is that this is an educational project, not a storefront. Keeping it free also keeps it simple and keeps you safe: with nothing to buy, there is no checkout to protect, no card number to store, and no payment dispute that could ever arise between us.

A charge that claims to be from us

Please read this carefully. The Lamplit Almanac will never telephone you, never send you an invoice, and never ask you to pay to remove a problem from your computer or your accounts. If you receive a call, email, or invoice claiming to be from The Lamplit Almanac and asking for payment, it is not from us — it is a scam.

Do not pay it, and do not share card or bank details. You can tell us about it at letters@lamplitalmanac.com, and you can report it to the U.S. Federal Trade Commission at reportfraud.ftc.gov.

Contact us

Any question about a charge or this policy can go to letters@lamplitalmanac.com.
