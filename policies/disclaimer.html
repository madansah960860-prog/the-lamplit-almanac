By the The Lamplit Almanac Editorial Team · Last reviewed: October 2026

Effective date: October 20, 2026. The following disclaimer governs your use of The Lamplit Almanac.

The Lamplit Almanac offers general educational information. It is written with care, but it cannot account for your exact situation.

General information only

The content on The Lamplit Almanac is for general education and is not a substitute for professional advice. Details change over time and vary from one person, product, home, or region to the next.

We write everything on The Lamplit Almanac in good faith and to the best of our understanding, and we correct mistakes promptly when they come to light. Even so, general information can never account for the particulars of your own situation, and it is no replacement for advice from someone who can consider your circumstances directly.

Think of what you read on The Lamplit Almanac as a well-meant starting point from a knowledgeable friend, not as instructions from a licensed professional who knows your particular circumstances. Two situations that look alike can need different answers, and only someone who can assess yours directly can say for certain.

On our history writing

The Lamplit Almanac is an educational journal. Our articles are written in our own words and are checked against reliable references, but history is a vast and sometimes contested subject, and reasonable sources occasionally differ on dates and details. We aim to present events evenhandedly and to note where matters are genuinely unsettled.

We cover historical public figures and well-documented events, and we quote only from works long in the public domain. If you find an error, we are grateful to be told so we can correct it. The Lamplit Almanac is an independent educational website and is not affiliated with any institution mentioned.

Where our links lead

When The Lamplit Almanac points you to another website, we choose official and reputable sources, but we do not control them and are not responsible for what they publish or how they change. Always confirm important details on the official source itself.

Not affiliated

Where brand or product names appear on The Lamplit Almanac, they belong to their owners and are used only to describe or teach. The Lamplit Almanac is an independent educational website and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any company mentioned.

Your decisions remain your own

You know your home, your health, your money, and your circumstances far better than any general guide ever could. For that reason, the choices you make after reading The Lamplit Almanac are yours, and the responsibility for them rests with you and the professionals you consult — not with this website.

Use your own judgment

Please treat what you read here as a starting point. When a decision matters, check current official sources and talk to a qualified professional. The Lamplit Almanac is not responsible for actions taken based on its general information.
