Privacy Policy

By the The Lamplit Almanac Editorial Team · Last reviewed: October 2026

Effective date: October 20, 2026. This privacy policy covers everything published on the The Lamplit Almanac website.

We built The Lamplit Almanac as a place to read and learn, not as a place that gathers data about its visitors. This page explains, in plain language, what little we handle and the choices you have.

What we collect

You can read every page on The Lamplit Almanac without signing in, and nothing on the site profiles you or follows you around the web. The only personal information we ever receive is the information you choose to send us — for example, the content of an email you write to letters@lamplitalmanac.com.

When you email The Lamplit Almanac, we see your email address and whatever you include in your message. We read it only to answer you, and we do not add you to any mailing list.

Preferences stored on your device

The Lamplit Almanac saves two small preferences in your browser's own local storage: the text size you pick with the "Text size" control, and whether you have dismissed the storage-notice bar. These stay on your device, are never sent to us or anyone else, and you can clear them at any time through your browser settings.

We do not sell or share your information

The Lamplit Almanac does not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with anyone. There is no advertising network, no third-party pixel, and no social login anywhere on lamplitalmanac.com.

Your privacy rights (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have specific rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act. The Lamplit Almanac honors these rights for every reader, wherever you live:

  • The right to know what personal information is collected, used, or disclosed.
  • The right to delete personal information we hold about you.
  • The right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • The right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information — which does not apply here, because we do nothing of the kind.
  • The right to limit the use of sensitive personal information.
  • The right not to be treated differently for exercising any of these rights.

Because the only personal information we ever hold is an email you sent us, exercising most of these rights is as simple as writing to letters@lamplitalmanac.com and asking us to delete that message.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

The Lamplit Almanac is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information, please write to letters@lamplitalmanac.com and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

If we change how The Lamplit Almanac handles information, we will update this page and change the effective date above before the change takes effect. We encourage you to glance at this page now and then.

Contact us

Questions about privacy can go to letters@lamplitalmanac.com. We aim to reply within two business days.
