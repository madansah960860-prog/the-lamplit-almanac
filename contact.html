Contact The Lamplit Almanac

By the The Lamplit Almanac Editorial Team · Last reviewed: October 2026

We read every letter ourselves, and nothing pleases us more than hearing from a curious reader. The best way to reach us is by email — there is no form to fill in and no account to create.

Write to us

Send your note to letters@lamplitalmanac.com. Tell us what you were reading and what is on your mind, and we will do our best to reply. We aim to answer within two business days.

Spotted a wrong date or a detail that needs correcting? Our editors are grateful for it at editor@lamplitalmanac.com. Accuracy matters in an almanac, and readers are our best proofreaders.

What we can and cannot do

  • We can point you to an article, add a subject to our list, or fix an error on a page.
  • We cannot do your research for you or settle scholarly disputes, though we are glad to point toward good public sources.
  • We offer no paid services and will never ask you for payment.

A few common questions

Is The Lamplit Almanac free?
Entirely. Every article is free to read, there is nothing to buy, and we take no payments and run no advertising.
Where do you get your facts?
We write in our own words from reliable references and check our facts carefully. Where history is genuinely uncertain, we say so.
Can I suggest a topic?
Please do. Reader suggestions often shape what we write next, and we keep a long and growing list of subjects to cover.
Why do some "on this day" entries seem to be missing?
Our collection grows over time. If a date you care about is not yet there, tell us; we are always glad to add well-documented events.
